# Privacy Policy – SharpStack

**Effective date:** August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SharpStack ("the App") handles information when you use the game.

## 1. Who we are

SharpStack is a mobile game published by the repository owner of:
`https://github.com/Step955/SharpStack`

If you have questions about privacy, use the contact information in section 10.

## 2. What SharpStack does

SharpStack is an arcade-style stacking game.  
Core gameplay is based on local interaction, score progression, and game session flow.

Based on current repository functionality, the App is designed to run primarily with local gameplay logic.

## 3. Data we collect

### 3.1 Data collected directly by the App
At this time, SharpStack is intended to minimize personal data processing.  
The app may store basic gameplay-related data locally on the device (for example settings, progress, or high score style values), depending on platform build behavior.

### 3.2 Data collected by third-party services (Advertising)
SharpStack may include Google AdMob for displaying ads now or in future releases.  
When ads are enabled, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as:

- Device identifiers (including advertising identifiers)
- Approximate location (derived from IP)
- App interaction and ad performance data
- Diagnostics and fraud-prevention signals

This data is processed by Google under Google’s own policies.

## 4. Why data is processed

Data processing (if applicable) is used for:

- Running and stabilizing the app
- Measuring performance and crashes
- Delivering and improving ads (AdMob)
- Preventing abuse and invalid traffic

## 5. Legal basis (where applicable)

Depending on user region and applicable law (e.g., GDPR/UK GDPR), processing may rely on:

- Legitimate interests (app security, diagnostics, fraud prevention)
- Consent (personalized advertising where required)
- Compliance with legal obligations

## 6. Children’s privacy

SharpStack is a game and may be used by broad audiences.  
If your release is directed to children or mixed audiences, you must configure Google Play and AdMob settings accordingly (age targeting, ad treatment, consent mode, and family policy compliance).

The publisher does not knowingly collect personal data directly from children beyond what is necessary for app operation and policy-compliant advertising delivery.

## 7. Data sharing

The app publisher does not sell personal data.  
Data may be shared with service providers strictly to operate the app (e.g., ad delivery, analytics/diagnostics tooling if enabled).

## 8. Data retention

- Local game data remains on the user device until removed by the user (for example by clearing app data or uninstalling).
- Third-party data retention is governed by the relevant provider (e.g., Google AdMob/Google services).
